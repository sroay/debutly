Rehearse your launch.

Debutly sends AI agents through your web app as its first customers. In a real browser they sign up, confirm their email, pick a plan, invite their team and use the product. Then they tell you what broke, and where they gave up.

How a rehearsal works

  1. Start a test copy of your app

    A staging site, or a throwaway copy built fresh in CI with test keys. Never production: the agents create accounts, submit forms and pay.

  2. Describe your customers

    Say who buys from you and what they should get done. Debutly casts them: owners with budgets, their teammates, and people who use the web differently.

  3. Let them use it

    Each agent gets its own browser and inbox, and works through your app a step at a time, reading what each page says and offers, as a person would.

  4. Read the notes

    Every problem comes with the steps to reproduce it, screenshots, and how many agents ran into it.

Notes from a rehearsal

From a run against a demo app. In the dashboard, each finding opens a replay of the moment it happened.

Blocker seen by 3 of 4 agents

Invite links expire before teammates open them

  1. The owner invites a teammate from Settings.
  2. The teammate opens the email a minute later.
  3. The page says "This link has expired", with no way to ask for a new one.

Major seen by the low-vision agent

The cookie banner covers the Pay button at 200% zoom

  1. Zoom the checkout page to 200%.
  2. Scroll to the card form.
  3. The banner sits over Pay, and its close button is off the screen.

Minor seen by the screen-reader agent

Plan buttons have no names

  1. Open Pricing with a screen reader.
  2. Move through the plans: each is announced only as "button".

Not counted as bugs

The test copy blocked a payment webhook and two outgoing emails, as it should. Failures that happened because of that are listed apart, so they never pile up in your bug list.

What gets rehearsed

Whole businesses, not just users
An owner signs up and pays; teammates accept invites and work in their own logins. Roles, seats and shared workspaces get tested too.
People who use the web differently
Agents act out access needs: one hears what a screen reader would and works by keyboard only; one works at 200% zoom; one is on a slow connection; one reads as a non-native English speaker. They point to problems for a person to check, and are not an accessibility audit.
What buyers think
Skeptical buyer agents read your landing and pricing pages next to your competitors' and say why they would say no. Compare two versions before you ship one.
Real bugs, not test-environment noise
If your app runs in a sandbox, Debutly reads what it blocked and keeps those failures out of your findings.
A replay of every run
The dashboard shows each agent's run step by step: what it saw, what it did, why, and what happened next.
Costs you can see
Every run is priced before it starts and can be capped. A quick run with a low-cost model costs a few cents.
